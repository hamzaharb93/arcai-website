Clinical Support

AI support where your team actually needs it.

ARCAI uses AI inside the workflow: dose support, trend detection, voice-note structuring, and early risk alerts. The goal is simple: less manual effort and clearer decisions during busy shifts.

Risk Assessment

Before a patient goes under, ARCAI checks breed, age, weight, bloodwork, and pre-existing conditions to flag anesthesia risk, so your team starts each case better prepared.

Voice Documentation

Speak in German or English during surgery. ARCAI transcribes and structures notes into clinical fields, then routes vitals, medications, and observations automatically so your hands stay sterile.

Early Warning System

Instead of waiting for a value to cross a threshold, ARCAI tracks vital trends and alerts your team when parameters start moving in the wrong direction, giving you time to act.

Shared Patient Record

One patient, one record across all three apps. When a patient moves from the OR to the ward, all data transfers with a single tap, with no re-entry and no transcription errors.

Drug Calculations

Auto-calculated doses by weight and species. CRI rates, fluid plans, and interaction flags are built into the drug selection workflow, reducing mental math under pressure.

Clinical Insights

Procedure duration, complication rates, and treatment compliance highlight patterns that help clinic managers make informed decisions about staffing, scheduling, and outcomes.

FAQ

Questions clinics usually ask before rollout

These are the practical questions we hear most often from clinic leadership, head nurses, and anesthesia teams.

1) How much extra work does this create for the team in daily routine?
The goal is less admin work, not more. Rollout focuses on replacing duplicate documentation steps. In onboarding, we map your current process and keep the team flow as close as possible to what already works.
2) How long does onboarding take before we can use it safely in real cases?
Most clinics start with a structured pilot phase and guided onboarding. Teams usually begin with selected workflows first, then expand once everyone is comfortable and the process is stable.
3) Do we need to change our whole clinic process to fit the software?
No. The setup is adapted to your existing workflow. The objective is to reduce friction and paper dependence while preserving clear responsibilities for vets, nurses, and reception staff.
4) Can ARCAI work with our existing clinic management system?
Yes, via API integration where available. This can include syncing key data such as upcoming surgery appointments so teams get earlier visibility without manual re-entry.
5) Who owns the clinical data?
Your clinic remains the data owner. ARCAI acts as a software platform that processes data for clinical operations according to agreed terms and legal requirements.
6) Is this compliant with German and EU data-protection expectations?
Data protection is addressed as a core rollout topic, including GDPR-related documentation, responsibilities, and technical safeguards. Compliance details are clarified with each clinic during implementation.
7) What happens if Wi-Fi is unstable or the internet goes down?
Operational reliability is planned during onboarding, including device setup and connectivity checks. Fallback procedures are defined with the clinic so teams can continue working safely during interruptions.
8) Can we control who can see or edit specific information?
Yes. Access can be organized by role and responsibility so teams see the data relevant to their task while maintaining accountability and traceability.
9) Can we track who changed what and when?
Yes. Clinical teams and leadership can review documentation history to support transparent handovers, quality assurance, and internal accountability.
10) How much training is required for vets and nurses?
Training is practical and role-based. The aim is fast confidence in daily tasks rather than long theoretical sessions, with support during the first live phase.
11) How does a pilot start for our clinic?
We begin with a workflow review, define a focused pilot scope, and align rollout steps with your team. Commercial terms are discussed after scope is clear so your clinic can decide with confidence.
12) What support do we get if something is unclear during a shift?
Support is structured around real clinical operations, especially in the early rollout stage. Response expectations and contact channels are defined with each clinic.
13) Does this replace veterinary judgment or protocol responsibility?
No. ARCAI is a clinical software tool that supports documentation and decision workflows. Medical responsibility remains with the attending veterinary professionals.
14) Can we start small and expand department by department?
Yes. Most clinics begin with a focused scope, then scale in phases once teams are stable and internal confidence is high.
15) How can our clinic evaluate fit before committing?
Book a workflow-focused demo. We review your current process, identify quick wins, and outline a realistic pilot plan so your team can decide with clarity.
